You're all set

DevSec Toolkit is installed. Here is how to get started in under a minute.

Start here

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin DevSec Toolkit so it is one click away.
  2. Open any website and click the DevSec Toolkit icon — the tools open in Chrome's side panel.
  3. Press Scan this page. Open a finding to see what it means and how to fix it. Press Add to report to keep it.
  4. Use the left sidebar to switch tools. On Report, download Markdown or HTML you can share or print to PDF.

What's inside

SidebarWhat it does
ScanChecks security headers (CSP, HSTS and more), mixed content, exposed secrets and API keys, tokens, source maps, outdated libraries, and (with your permission) cookie flags.
JWTDecodes a token and points out weak spots: no expiry, alg: none, risky header fields.
ConvertBase64, URL, HTML and hex encode and decode, SHA hashes, and a Unix timestamp converter.
JSONFormat, minify, sort and validate, with the exact line and column of any error.
RegexTest patterns with live matches and capture groups. Slow patterns are stopped safely.
ReportCollect findings and export a client-ready report.

Our promises

  • Everything stays on your computer. No accounts, no analytics, no servers.
  • Passive only. A scan reads the page you are on and its own script files. It sends no attack payloads and never contacts other sites.
  • You are in control. It runs only when you click it. Cookie checking asks for permission, for one site at a time.

Please test responsibly

Only scan websites and applications that you own, or where you have clear written permission to test. Testing systems without permission may be illegal in your country. Results come from automated, passive checks, so review each finding by hand before you act on it or report it.
